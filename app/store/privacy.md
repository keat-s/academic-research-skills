# ARS Studio — Privacy Policy (template)

_Last updated: replace before publishing._

ARS Studio is built on the Academic Research Skills suite. This template
describes how the reference implementation handles data. Adapt it to your actual
deployment and host it at a public URL (both app stores require one).

## What we collect
- **Account:** your email and (optionally) a display name, to sign you in.
- **Content you create:** your conversations and uploaded document text are
  stored so you can return to them. Original uploaded files are **not** retained
  — only extracted text.
- **Coarse usage events:** counts of actions (e.g. "a chat happened", "an export
  happened") with no message content and no personal data, to understand load.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not store your bring-your-own-key (it lives only in your browser and is
  forwarded directly to the model provider).
- We do not run third-party tracking. Ads, if a deployer enables them, use a
  non-tracking provider.

## AI processing
When you use the free cloud models, your prompts are sent to OpenRouter and the
selected model provider to generate a response, subject to their policies. Using
a local model (Ollama) keeps inference entirely on your device. Citation
grounding sends short search queries to Crossref, OpenAlex, and Semantic Scholar.

## Data retention & deletion
You can delete individual conversations and uploads in the app. Contact the
operator to delete your account and associated data.

## Children
Not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
