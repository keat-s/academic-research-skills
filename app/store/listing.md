ARS Studio is an AI-native workspace for academic work, built on the open
Academic Research Skills suite. Run 25 research, writing, and peer-review
workflows — deep research, literature reviews, paper drafting, multi-perspective
review, Socratic planning, and a full research-to-paper pipeline.

- Free AI features powered by open models. Bring your own key for premium
  models, or run fully offline with a local model.
- Citation grounding retrieves real references (Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic
  Scholar) so the AI cites actual DOIs instead of fabricating them.
- Attach PDFs and documents for review, revision, and citation checking.
- Export to Markdown, HTML, LaTeX, RTF, DOCX, and PDF.
- Works on web, mobile, and desktop.

AI is your copilot, not the pilot — it handles the grunt work so you focus on
the thinking. Always verify citations before use.
